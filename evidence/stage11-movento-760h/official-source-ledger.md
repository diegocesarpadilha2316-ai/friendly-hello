## Transcribed manufacturer facts

The official Blum drawer-building page states:

> “SKW (internal drawer width) = LW (internal cabinet width) – 42 mm.”

> “Drawer length (= drawer side length): NL – 10 mm.”

> “Please note that the drawer side must not be wider than 16 mm.”

> “The recess must be at least 12 mm high in order to conceal the runner and must be maximum 15 mm deep.”

The official 2024/2025 catalogue page [3] additionally renders `SKW = LW – 42 +0.0 –1.5`, `SKL = NL – 10`, `12–15`, `max 16`, and `Use drilling template T65.1000.02`. It also marks `+12 mm with side stabilisation` and `+3 mm with depth adjustment` as optional planning additions rather than base drawer equations.

The official 2024/2025 overview [2] identifies MOVENTO 760H as a 40 kg dynamic carrying-capacity runner family. Page [4] identifies the official attachment hardware references: chipboard screw Ø3.5 × 15 mm, part no. 609.1500; system screw Ø6 × 14.5 mm, part no. 661.1450.HG. The same page shows that the attachment positions depend on nominal length `NL` and distinguishes standard runner attachment from the separate base-fixing variant.

## Facts intentionally not assumed

The official sources gathered here do not, by themselves, establish a complete manufacturer-approved rule for every carcass material, every back-panel construction, every front thickness, every side-stabilisation choice, or every machining coordinate needed by the Dioris engine. Those values remain `UNKNOWN` or `INCOMPLETE` until an applicable Blum technical drawing/configurator result is archived and mapped.

The Stage 11 pilot must therefore use a single explicit nominal length and a selected MOVENTO 760H variant, while keeping the following separate from the manufacturer facts: carcass-derived `LW`, computed `SKW`, computed `SKL`, drawer side thickness, box height, box depth, front thickness, and all optional accessories.

## Provenance rule

Only the URLs in this ledger may be cited as manufacturer evidence for the Stage 11 pilot. If a value cannot be traced to one of these official Blum sources or to an existing Dioris rule explicitly labelled as a furniture rule, it must not be promoted to a verified industrial parameter.
