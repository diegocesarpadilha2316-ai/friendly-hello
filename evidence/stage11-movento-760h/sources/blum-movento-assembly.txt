MOVENTO
Easy assembly and installation
MOVENTO's simple installation and adjustments help make your project plans a reality.
4 dimensions for greater precision
With MOVENTO, you can adjust the side-to-side, height, tilt and depth of the drawer front. There is no need to modify the drawer, and you get perfectly aligned fronts quickly, easily and without tools!
Assembly stays the same
The MOVENTO runner system gives you an extensive range of options, and yet assembly is always the same. Regardless of whether you combine the runner with BLUMOTION, SERVO-DRIVE, or
TIP-ON BLUMOTION
the drilling pattern and mounting positions of the runner remain the same.
Support every step of the way
For MOVENTO, we also offer services and assembly devices for all your day-to-day tasks.The concealed runner template for drawer preparation, the universal drilling template for runner installation, and the front gap template for
TIP-ON BLUMOTION
for MOVENTO all ensure that assembly is quick, easy and precise. Thanks to the EASY ASSEMBLY app, 4-dimensional adjustment of the front takes no time at all.
